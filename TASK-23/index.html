OUR STORY

Built around curiosity, community and connection.

Greenwood Public Library has been part of the neighbourhood since 1983. From children's reading programs to quiet study spaces, the library welcomes people of every age.

Today, Greenwood combines traditional library services with free Wi-Fi, digital resources, community events and modern learning spaces.
